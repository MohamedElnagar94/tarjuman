Privacy policy

Tarjuman — Arabic subtitles & dubbing for any video · Last updated 9 November 2026

  • The extension talks only to the Tarjuman app on your own computer (127.0.0.1 / localhost).
  • There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives nothing.
  • Your data is never sold or shared for any other purpose.

What the extension processes, and where it goes

The extension does not read pages you don't translate, does not record your browsing, and keeps no history.

What the local Tarjuman app may contact

The Tarjuman app runs on your computer and does the actual work. To do it, it may contact these services directly from your computer:

  • YouTube — to read a video's captions and details, and to download audio or video when you ask it to transcribe or dub a video.
  • Google Translate and MyMemory — to translate caption text. Only the text being translated is sent.
  • Microsoft Edge text-to-speech voices — to synthesize the dubbed voice. Only the sentence being spoken is sent. An offline voice (Piper) is used when it is installed or the online voices are unavailable.
  • An AI provider you configure yourself (optional) — for example Anthropic (Claude) or an OpenAI-compatible service, only if you enter your own API key in the app. Then the caption text of the videos you choose to process with AI is sent to that provider. Your key stays on your computer.
  • Sites you give the app a link to — when you ask it to download a video from another site.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Speech recognition (Whisper) runs entirely on your computer. The app keeps its cache, your corrections and your files in its own data/ folder, which you can delete at any time.

Permissions

The extension asks only for what it needs: storage for settings, temporary access to the current tab when you click it (activeTab), showing the subtitle overlay (scripting), and audio capture for live mode (tabCapture, offscreen). It can reach only 127.0.0.1 and localhost. On sites other than YouTube it asks you for that one site, and you can remove sites at any time in its settings.

Children

Tarjuman is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
